Scope: **only** Apple’s two first-party cases. Do not conflate with third-party
products or MagSafe desk stands branded “Duo.”

**117.8 × 164.6 × 5.2 mm**, **254 g** — see `device.md`.

## MagSafe / Qi2 through both cases

Apple Newsroom: both cases are MagSafe, Qi2 **25 W**, and Qi-compatible with
built-in magnets aligned to Duo.

| Case | Charging path (Apple Store wording) |
|---|---|
| Folio | MagSafe / Qi2 25W / Qi **on the outside or behind the open kickstand** |
| Duo Case | MagSafe snap-on, or Qi2 25W / Qi pad (no kickstand path) |

General MagSafe case thickness ceiling from Guidelines (not Duo-case measured):
uniform rear over array **≤ 2.1 mm** (Apple recommends **2.0 mm**). Whether
Folio/Case meet that is **unverified** from consumer pages.

## Folio with Kickstand — envelope

Construction highlights (Apple):

- Integrated kickstand with a marketed **range / wide range of angles**; snaps
  back when unused
- Cover over the **entire outer display** when closed (Store imagery / alt text)
- Magnetic attach; MagSafe recess usable under open kickstand

Measured / secondary (label carefully):

| Observation | Confidence |
|---|---|
| Folio mass ~**58 g** | One physical review (iPhone Mania) |
| Kickstand ~**90°** open | Same review — Apple does not publish degrees |
| MagSafe-shaped recess under stand | Same review + Store “behind kickstand” claim |
| Legacy MagSafe banks overhang ~**15 mm** on short edge | Same review |
| Magnetic attach; little/no side wrap vs hard case | MacRumors / PhoneArena |
| Smart Cover wake/sleep | **Unverified** secondary — not on Apple Store pages |

Dock implications:

1. Reserve volume for kickstand deploy + charger nest under stand.
2. Closed Folio hides outer display under flap — do not assume always-visible
   cover screen.
3. Fabric compliance differs from polycarbonate Case friction/lips.

## Duo Case — envelope

Construction highlights (Apple):

- Explicitly **two-part** polycarbonate wrapping **both halves** front-to-back
- MagSafe through camera-half back
- Store imagery: **titanium hinge exposed** when folded; outer edges covered

Secondary reporting (MacRumors / hands-on):

- Adhesive strips along inside edges because open halves are thin
- MagSafe magnets in the **camera-half piece only**; cover-display half has none
- MagSafe attach also magnetic; adhesive aids retention of the thin shells

Dock implications:

1. Size for edge lips / two-shell stack, not bare 5.2 / 11.3 mm alone.
2. Never crush the exposed hinge cover with clamps sized only for glass.
3. No under-kickstand MagSafe path — conventional through-case MagSafe only.

## Installed thickness / weight — unknown

Apple publishes **no** installed case thickness or weight for either SKU.

**Do not use** Amazon / carrier “product dimensions” or weights as MagSafe
air-gap inputs — they track packaging (examples seen: Folio width 0.57 in,
Case 0.72 in).

FoldifyCase ~**1 mm** wall / ~**+2 mm** closed estimate is **derived**, not
measured on Apple’s case.

## Dock claim language

Declare supported states explicitly, for example:

- Bare closed MagSafe
- Bare open with ledge
- Folio closed / Folio kickstand-propped (with under-stand MagSafe clearance)
- Duo Case closed / open

A single “works with Apple Duo cases” claim is insufficient — Folio and Case
are incompatible envelopes.
