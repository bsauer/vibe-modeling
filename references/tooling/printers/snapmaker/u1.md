# Snapmaker U1

Provenance: user-confirmed and session-validated during 2026-10-21 /
2026-10-22 iPhone Duo plate and MagSafe stand work. See
`references/tooling/README.md`.

## Build volume

| Axis | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X / Y / Z | **270 × 270 × 270 mm** | User-stated printable plate / volume for this workspace’s U1 |

Do not treat the older host default of **220 mm** (used by many cyberdeck /
Cicero plans) as a U1 limit. Those designs encode their own `print_bed` /
`print_volume_*` parameters.

## Bed clearance (Snapmaker Orca)

Objects placed **flush** to the 270 mm bed edges trigger Snapmaker Orca
“too close to the edges” warnings.

Practical placement used successfully:

- Leave about **5 mm** clearance on each side that approaches a bed limit.
- Example: a **260 mm** wide part centered on the 270 mm bed at world
  **X 5–265 mm**.

A part that is exactly 270 mm wide can sit at X 0–270 mm geometrically, but the
slicer may still warn; prefer a slight trim or inset when Orca complains.

## Nozzles and time

Observed on a ~12 mm tall plate (~260 × 173 footprint):

| Setup | Observed outcome |
|---|---|
| **0.2 mm** nozzle, **0.08 mm** layers, flow capped near **2 mm³/s** | ~**1 day 5 hours** estimate; speed fields barely apply |
| **0.4 mm** nozzle, **0.20 mm** High Quality (first layer 0.25 mm) | Primary time cut; typically hours instead of ~29 h for that plate |

Guidance:

1. Prefer the printer’s **0.4 mm** High Quality preset for large structural or
   utility plates unless fine detail requires 0.2 mm.
2. On 0.2 mm, raising layer height to **0.12–0.16 mm** is the next best lever.
3. Wall count multiplies cost on parts with many full-height holes; three
   0.4 mm walls usually beat four thin 0.2 mm walls for strength/time.
4. Disable **prime / wipe tower** for single-color U1 jobs.

## Trimming parts to the U1 bed

When shortening a part that has **sloped rims** and a **flat top**:

- A simple plane cut near the outer end can slice through the **slope**, leaving
  a short vertical face (observed ~8.8 mm tall instead of full 12 mm height).
- To **keep the sloped perimeter**, remove length from the **flat top** band and
  close the gap so the original slopes move inward.
- Confirm the cut intent against marked screenshots before writing a new 3MF.
