# Design around Apple’s 8.9 mm cylinders

Apple still sells **four** Apple Pencil models new on the US catalog—**Pencil Pro**, **Pencil (USB-C)**, **Pencil (2nd generation)**, and **Pencil (1st generation)**—and every one shares an official **8.9 mm (0.35 in)** diameter, so accessory CAD should start from that cylinder plus model-specific length and charge UX rather than from MagSafe or Wikipedia geometry. Official public documentation stops at length × diameter × weight: Apple publishes **no** Pencil dimensional drawings, tip thread specs, flat-face chord widths, or Pencil-side magnet arrays, and the public Accessory Design Guidelines mention Pencil mainly for screen-overlay coexistence (≤ **0.095 mm**), not for holders or docks. Magnetic behavior splits sharply: Pro and 2nd gen **attach, pair, and wirelessly charge** on the iPad long-edge magnet rail; USB-C **attaches for storage only** and charges through a sliding USB-C end; 1st gen uses Lightning plus a removable magnetic cap and has no side-rail charge. For iPhone Duo, Apple lists **only Pencil (USB-C)**—with software enablement framed as “coming later this year”—so Duo-facing accessories must assume cable charge and **no** iPad-style Pencil edge magnets on the phone. One official Tips 4-pack (`MX763AM/A`) fits all four pens, which simplifies spare-tip trays once tips are calipered. The practical design envelope is therefore Apple’s published cylinders, iPad device-side magnet/flux keep-outs for on-rail products, and measured clearances for everything Apple leaves unpublished.

## Four catalog pens still share one diameter

As of late October 2026, Apple’s overview and iPad accessories category still offer Buy links for Pro (`MX2D3` / `MX2D3AM/A`), USB-C (`MUWA3` / `MUWA3AM/A`), 2nd gen (`MXN43` / `MXN43AM/A`), and 1st gen (`MYQW3` / `MYQW3AM/A`), plus Tips (`MX763AM/A`) and the USB-C to Apple Pencil Adapter (`MWML3AM/A`) ([Apple Pencil overview](https://www.apple.com/apple-pencil/); [iPad Accessories · Apple Pencil](https://www.apple.com/shop/ipad/accessories/apple-pencil)). US list prices are **$129** (Pro and 2nd gen), **$79** (USB-C), **$99** (1st gen), **$19** (tips), and **$9** (adapter) ([Pro product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mx2d3am/a/apple-pencil-pro); [USB-C product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/muwa3am/a/apple-pencil-usb-c); [2nd gen product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mxn43am/a/apple-pencil-2nd-generation); [1st gen product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/myqw3am/a/apple-pencil-1st-generation); [Tips](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mx763am/a/apple-pencil-tips-4-pack); [Adapter](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mwml3am/a/usb-c-to-apple-pencil-adapter)). Marketing emphasizes Pro and USB-C, but 1st and 2nd gen remain live catalog SKUs for older iPads rather than discontinued bodies ([Select Apple Pencil](https://www.apple.com/shop/select-apple-pencil)).

Support tech-specs and shop pages agree on the envelopes that matter for holders: Pro and 2nd gen are **166 mm** long at **8.9 mm** diameter (weights **19.15 g** and **18.2 g**); USB-C is **155 mm** × **8.9 mm** (**20.5 g**); 1st gen is **175.7 mm tip-to-cap** × **8.9 mm** (**20.7 g**) ([Support 120123](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123); [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318); [Support 111889](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111889); [Support 111920](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111920)). Secondary tables that list USB-C at **7.4 mm (0.29 in)** conflict with Apple and must be ignored for CAD ([Wikipedia · Apple Pencil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Pencil); contradicted by [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318)). Feature differentiation is sharp enough to drive accessory forks: only Pro adds squeeze, barrel roll, haptics, and Find My; only USB-C among current pens lacks pressure sensitivity; only Pro and 2nd gen wirelessly pair and charge when magnetically attached; USB-C magnetically attaches for storage and pairs/charges by cable ([apple.com/apple-pencil](https://www.apple.com/apple-pencil/); [Support 120123](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123); [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318)). FCC model IDs (**A2538** / BCGA2538 for Pro; **A3085** / BCGA3085 for USB-C) supply external photos useful for qualitative flat-face and slider cues, not dimensioned CAD ([fccid.io BCGA2538](https://fccid.io/BCGA2538); [fccid.io BCGA3085](https://fccid.io/BCGA3085)).

## Guidelines cover overlays; drawings cover iPad rails

Public Accessory Design Guidelines (Release R31, updated 2026-09-21) download from Apple Developer and address physical compatibility across Apple product families, with some interaction aspects reserved for MFi and the Accessory Interface Specification ([Accessories – Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/); [Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)). For Pencil specifically, the Guidelines require that screen overlays not degrade Multi-Touch, Apple Pencil, or sensors; overlays **should not exceed 0.095 mm** thickness to support Pencil; non-glossy surfaces may accelerate tip wear; and accessory trackpads must not degrade Pencil performance ([Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)). There is **no** public ADG chapter for Pencil body mounts, tip threads, Pencil magnet keepouts, or holder clearances. MagSafe and Qi magnetic-attach chapters in the same PDF apply to MagSafe/Qi accessories, **not** to Apple Pencil side-mount charging ([Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)).

Apple’s Dimensional Drawings catalog lists iPads, iPhones, Watches, AirPods, MagSafe chargers, Vision Pro, and similar devices—but **no Apple Pencil model**—so the only Apple-authoritative Pencil geometry remains the tech-spec cylinders above ([Download Dimensional Drawings](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/dimensional-drawings/)). Useful Pencil-adjacent keepouts live on **iPad** drawings: for example, the iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) package includes magnet detail, polarity tables, and magnetic-flux keep-outs such as a **3 mm thick** flux keep-out with **maximum 0.25 mT** in a cross-hatched region and **maximum 5 mT** in a hatched region, extending above and below product thickness near the long-edge array used with Pencil ([iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) dimensional drawing](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/accessories/dimensional-drawings/ipad-pro-13-inch-m5.pdf)). Support pairing guidance to center Pro/2nd gen on the magnetic connector and to remove cases that block pairing confirms that third-party covers can interrupt attach and charge ([If you can't pair Apple Pencil with your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT205236); [Pair and charge Apple Pencil Pro](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/pair-and-charge-apple-pencil-pro-ipada6b9b799/ipados)). MFi Accessory Interface Specification content that might add Pencil magnet or tip specs was not accessible without program membership and must be treated as gated rather than assumed.

## Magnets, flats, and tips force measurement beyond Apple’s PDFs

Apple confirms a matte finish and a **flat side** that magnetically attaches Pro, 2nd gen, and USB-C to the iPad edge, with Pro and 2nd gen pairing and charging on that rail and USB-C using the same paradigm for storage/sleep only ([Apple Newsroom 2023-10-17](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/10/apple-introduces-new-apple-pencil-bringing-more-value-and-choice-to-the-lineup/); [Support 120123](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123); [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318); [Support 111889](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111889)). Apple publishes **no** flat-face chord width, flat length along the barrel, Pencil magnet pitch/polarity, or attach force. Community CAD defaults that assume roughly **7.5 mm** flat width are editable model parameters, not Apple measurements, and must be verified on hardware before locking production geometry ([SpeakCAD Apple Pencil Pro grip sleeve](https://speakcad.com/library/apple-pencil-pro-ergonomic-grip-sleeve-tpu)).

USB-C Pencil reveals its charge/pair port through a **permanently attached sliding cap** with magnetic open/closed detents; Apple documents the behavior and the need to slide the end open before connecting a USB-C cable, but not travel distance in millimeters ([Apple Newsroom 2023-10-17](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/10/apple-introduces-new-apple-pencil-bringing-more-value-and-choice-to-the-lineup/); [iPad User Guide · pair and charge USB-C](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/pair-and-charge-apple-pencil-usb-c-ipadc3d88218/ipados); [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318)). Closed overall length is the published **155 mm**; open length equals that plus unknown slide travel. First-generation Pencil instead uses a **removable** magnetically attached Lightning cap, a different accessory failure mode (lost-cap pockets versus trapped sliders) ([Support 111920](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111920)).

Official replacement tips are the **Apple Pencil Tips – 4 pack** (`MX763AM/A`), compatible with Pro, 2nd gen, USB-C, and 1st gen, with four tips in the box and no published tip OD, tip length, or thread designation ([Tips product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mx763am/a/apple-pencil-tips-4-pack)). First gen ships with an extra tip; Pro/2nd gen/USB-C serial numbers sit under the removable tip, confirming an unscrew interface without specifying pitch or major diameter ([Support 111920](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111920); [Support 109031](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109031)). Community replacement guides describe counterclockwise removal onto a male metal prong and a paper-thin gap when seated correctly; pressure sensing lives in the shaft, not the plastic tip ([iFixit Tip Replacement Guide](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Apple+Pencil+(1st+Generation)+Tip+Replacement/154444); [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/how-to/change-apple-pencil-tip); [Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/255476298)). Spare-tip trays can share **one** cavity family across the lineup once the official tip body is calipered; writing-point marketing figures from retailers are not a storage envelope.

Apple publishes no holder clearance. Maker practice that has been stated with fit outcomes converges on starting from **8.9 mm** plus print clearance: roughly **9.1–9.3 mm** ID for rigid PLA/PETG cylindrical pockets, nearer **9.0–9.1 mm** for compliant TPU, with multi-fit variants common because printer tolerance dominates ([Printables – Apple Pencil Desk Stand](https://www.printables.com/model/108011-apple-pencil-desk-stand); [SpeakCAD grip sleeve](https://speakcad.com/library/apple-pencil-pro-ergonomic-grip-sleeve-tpu); [Thingiverse plastorub case](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4766666); [MakerWorld ApplePenHolder](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2015618-applepenholder)). Length forks matter more than diameter forks: Pro/2nd gen **166 mm**, USB-C **155 mm**, Gen1 **~176 mm** tip-to-cap. For magnetic charge on Pro/2nd gen, intervening thickness or ferrous hardware over the flat face is the failure mode—not demagnetization of the magnets by PLA/PETG/TPU. Commercial grip makers variously instruct “remove grip before charging” or claim cutouts that preserve charge; those claims conflict and must be validated against the Batteries widget rather than trusted from marketing ([UPPERCASE NimbleGrip](https://gouppercase.com/products/nimblegrip); [Paperlike Pencil Grips](https://paperlike.com/products/pencil-grips)). Tip-up storage or tip cavities with clearance protect the replaceable tip and the sensors behind it when a Pencil is perched on an iPad edge ([Printables sleeve 1793197](https://www.printables.com/model/1793197-apple-pencil-sleeve-2nd-generation-pro-usb-c)).

## Duo needs USB-C Pencil storage, not MagSafe Pencil charging

iPhone Duo technical specifications state that both displays **support Apple Pencil (USB-C)**; USB-C Pencil tech specs and the shop compatibility list explicitly include **iPhone Models → iPhone Duo**; Pro, 2nd gen, and 1st gen tech-specs list **iPad models only** ([iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/); [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318); [Support 120123](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123); [Support 111889](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111889); [Support 111920](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111920)). Apple Newsroom and marketing FAQ language add that Duo will work with Pencil (USB-C) **later this year** (calendar 2026), so product design can lock the model while treating software enablement timing as still rolling ([Apple Newsroom 2026-09-09](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/); [iPhone Duo](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/)). The older Support compatibility article that lists only iPads for all four pens and omits Duo is narrower than the USB-C and Duo primary pages and should not override them for phone accessory decisions ([Support 108937](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108937)).

That combination implies a clean accessory rule: Duo-facing docks and cases store and charge **USB-C Pencil** by cable after the sliding end opens; they must **not** be designed as if Duo had an iPad long-edge Pencil charge rail or as if Pencil Pro were supported. Duo MagSafe/Qi2 attach on the phone is a separate rear-half power/accessory system and is not Apple’s documented Pencil attach/charge path. On-iPad case and stand products that preserve Pro/2nd gen wireless charge (or USB-C magnetic storage) must instead follow the matching iPad dimensional drawing’s magnet and flux keep-outs and leave the Pencil flat face free of thick or ferromagnetic intervening material ([iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) dimensional drawing](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/accessories/dimensional-drawings/ipad-pro-13-inch-m5.pdf); [HT205236](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT205236)). Screen protectors on Duo or iPad that will be used with Pencil remain under the ADG **0.095 mm** overlay ceiling ([Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)).

## Conclusion

Accessory design for current Apple Pencils is constrained less by missing models than by missing mechanical drawings: the public stack gives authoritative **8.9 mm** cylinders, four charge/pair UX variants, universal tip interchangeability, and device-side iPad magnet keep-outs—while tip threads, flat chords, slider travel, and Pencil magnet arrays stay measurement problems or MFi-gated territory. The highest-leverage design decisions are therefore to reject secondary USB-C diameter figures, fork products on **length and charge path** rather than OD, treat Duo as **USB-C-only** mechanical storage plus cable charge, and treat MagSafe chapters as orthogonal to Pencil side-rail work. Until Apple publishes Pencil drawings or MFi packs fill the gaps, production-ready holders and tip trays should lock geometry only after calipers and on-device charge/attach tests against the exact SKUs those accessories claim to serve.
