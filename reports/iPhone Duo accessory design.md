# Map Duo envelopes before CAD

Apple’s published Size and Weight lock the only trustworthy chassis envelopes for accessory CAD: **117.8 × 164.6 × 5.2 mm** open and **117.8 × 84.1 × 11.3 mm** closed at **254 g**, with MagSafe and Qi2 wireless charging each rated up to **25 W** on a single rear camera-half magnet array. Those figures, plus qualitative keepouts for the hinge, USB-C, Touch ID side button, Camera Control, and camera plateau, are enough to prototype closed MagSafe docks and two-pose cradles today. What is still missing for precision fit—public iPhone Duo dimensional drawings, MagSafe ring center coordinates, official camera-bump height, and measured phone-plus-case stack-ups—has not appeared on Apple’s Accessory Design Guidelines dimensional-drawings index as of late October 2026. Apple’s two official cases (**iPhone Duo Folio with Kickstand**, SKUs MKR64/MKR74 at $129; **iPhone Duo Case**, SKUs MKR84/MKR94 at $79) define two incompatible accessory envelopes, not one “works with Apple cases” claim. MagSafe Attach magnet geometry, pull-force targets, and case-thickness ceilings are public in Apple’s Accessory Design Guidelines PDF; passive 3D-printed mounts and non-electronic cases do not require MFi enrollment, while active Qi2/MagSafe chargers require Qi certification and separate MagSafe licensing for branded claims.

## Apple locks open and closed envelopes at 5.2 mm and 11.3 mm

Apple’s Technical Specifications page is the primary mechanical source for the foldable chassis. In the open book pose the device measures **117.8 mm** height by **164.6 mm** width by **5.2 mm** depth; closed passport-style it keeps the same **117.8 mm** height, collapses to **84.1 mm** width, and thickens to **11.3 mm**, with mass **254 g** in either state ([Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/)). That geometry is landscape-dominant when open and short-and-wide when closed, so slab-era MagSafe wallets and batteries sized for taller iPhones overhang the **117.8 mm** short edge—an observation consistent across accessory fit reporting ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2281471/iphone-duo-which-accessories-fit/); [iPhone Mania Folio review](https://iphone-mania.jp/iphone18-607603/)). PhoneArena’s aggregator listing adds an unofficial open thickness of about **10.4 mm** including the camera bump; that figure is useful only as a provisional keepout until caliper measurement or an Apple drawing exists ([PhoneArena Apple iPhone Duo](https://www.phonearena.com/phones/Apple-iPhone-Duo_id12657)).

Displays and materials set secondary keepouts rather than envelope dimensions. The inner OLED is marketed as **7.6-inch** (rectangular **7.58 in**, **1878 × 2670** at **430 ppi**) with a nano-texture finish; the outer display is **5.4-inch** (rectangular **5.36 in**, **1398 × 2034** at **460 ppi**); both support ProMotion up to **120 Hz** ([Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/)). The enclosure is Grade 5 titanium with Ceramic Shield 2 on the outer front, Ceramic Shield on the back, a 3D-printed titanium hinge cover, and **IP68** rating—useful for finish and hinge-contact assumptions, not for wall thickness ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/); [Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/)). Pre-announce rumor thicknesses near **9–9.5 mm** folded and screen diagonals near **5.5″ / 7.8″** are obsolete relative to shipping Apple numbers and must not drive CAD ([MacRumors pre-launch expectations](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/iphone-fold-launch-pricing-what-to-expect/)).

Controls and ports define qualitative clearance zones until Duo-specific Accessory Design Guidelines sheets ship. Volume buttons sit on the top edge; USB-C and stereo speakers occupy the bottom and top/bottom acoustic paths; the right edge carries Touch ID in the side button plus Camera Control; there is no Action button and no physical SIM tray (eSIM only worldwide) ([Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/); [MacRumors roundup](https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/iphone-duo/)). Rear optics are a dual **48 MP** Fusion Main and Ultra Wide pair on a camera plateau with sapphire covers and True Tone flash; the outer face carries a **12 MP** Center Stage camera; the inner FaceTime camera is under-display ([Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/); [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/)). Accessories must leave the hinge spine free to flex, avoid clamping the exposed titanium hinge cover, and keep hard pressure off the crease—Apple’s own two-part case pattern leaves the hinge largely visible when folded ([Apple Duo Case product page](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr94zm/a/iphone-duo-case-sand)).

## MagSafe lives on one half and tips upright stands when open

Apple confirms MagSafe and Qi2 wireless charging up to **25 W**, listing a magnet array, alignment magnet, Accessory Identification NFC, and magnetometer on the phone specs page ([Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/)). Wired USB-C can reach about **50%** charge in roughly **20 minutes** with a **60 W+** adapter; MagSafe wireless reaches about **50%** in roughly **30 minutes** with a **35 W+** adapter and MagSafe Charger ([Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/); [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/)). Apple does not publish magnet-ring center coordinates, outer diameter on the phone, or NFC offsets for Duo, and the public dimensional-drawings index still lists other recent iPhones without an iPhone Duo entry ([Apple Developer dimensional drawings](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/dimensional-drawings/)).

Third-party hands-on and geometry reporting converge on a single practical rule: the MagSafe/Qi2 charging face sits on the **rear camera half only**, under the camera plateau, because the other outer face is the cover display ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2281471/iphone-duo-which-accessories-fit/); [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/09/09/contrary-to-early-rumors-iphone-duo-does-have-magsafe-charging/)). When the phone is unfolded, upright MagSafe stands can tip from asymmetric mass hanging off one half; MacRumors’ reporting recommends closed as the safest upright MagSafe pose, flat chargers to avoid rotation issues, and wider stands with a ledge for open use ([MacRumors MagSafe tip-over](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/21/iphone-duo-magsafe-charging/)). Hinge closure uses a separate integrated magnet array for the closed feel and must not be confused with the MagSafe charging ring ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/)).

For dock bore design around Apple’s puck rather than the phone, ChargerLAB’s measurements of the **25 W** MagSafe Charger (A2580) give panel diameter about **55.38–55.5 mm** and thickness about **4.45–4.53 mm**, while Apple publishes MagSafe Charger dimensional drawings for mount keep-ins ([ChargerLAB 25W review](https://www.chargerlab.com/review-of-apple-25w-magsafe-charger); [Apple Developer dimensional drawings](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/dimensional-drawings/)). Community mockups on MakerWorld that reuse Apple’s overall open/folded millimeters explicitly warn that they are visualization aids and must not drive precision case or mount lips ([MakerWorld size mockups](https://makerworld.com/en/models/3285874-iphone-duo-size-dimension-mockups-open-folded)). Printables hits titled “MagSafe Duo” usually mean Apple’s discontinued dual MagSafe desk charger, not the foldable phone, and are the wrong envelope entirely ([Printables MagSafe Duo charger stand](https://www.printables.com/model/383203-magsafe-duo-charger-stand-for-iphone-apple-watch-v)).

## Folio and Duo Case force two incompatible dock envelopes

Apple sells exactly two first-party Duo cases, both MagSafe/Qi2 **25 W**/Qi-compatible with built-in magnets aligned to the phone ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/)). The **iPhone Duo Folio with Kickstand** is TechWoven fabric at **$129** in Taupe (**MKR64ZM/A**) and Navy Blue (**MKR74ZM/A**); the **iPhone Duo Case** is matte polycarbonate at **$79** in Sand (**MKR94ZM/A**) and Navy Blue (**MKR84ZM/A**) ([Apple Folio Taupe](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe); [Apple Duo Case Sand](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr94zm/a/iphone-duo-case-sand); [Mac Observer SKU table](https://www.macobserver.com/news/iphone-duo-folio-case-prices/)). Compatibility on both Store pages is **iPhone Duo only**; the box contains only the case ([Apple Folio Taupe](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe); [Apple Duo Case Sand](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr94zm/a/iphone-duo-case-sand)).

Construction and charging paths diverge enough that a single “works with Apple Duo cases” claim fails. The Folio’s Store copy states you can snap a MagSafe, Qi2 **25 W**, or Qi-certified charger **on the outside or behind the open kickstand**, implying a MagSafe nesting cavity under the deployed stand leaf ([Apple Folio Taupe](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe)). Store imagery describes a cover over the entire outer display when closed and a kickstand that snaps back when unused, supporting a marketed “range of angles” without published degree stops ([Apple Folio Taupe](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe); [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/)). A Folio-only physical review measured the case at **58 g**, observed kickstand opening to about **90°**, noted MagSafe-shaped recess under the stand, confirmed magnetic attach with no side walls, and saw legacy MagSafe banks overhang about **15 mm** past the short Folio edge ([iPhone Mania Folio review](https://iphone-mania.jp/iphone18-607603/)). MacRumors and PhoneArena report the Folio attaches magnetically without adhesive and does not wrap the sides the way the hard case does ([MacRumors adhesive article](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/16/iphone-duo-case-adhesive/); [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/Apples-new-79-iPhone-Duo-case-requires-actual-glue-to-stay-on-your-phone_id184121)).

The Duo Case is an explicitly **two-part** polycarbonate shell that wraps both halves front-to-back for drop protection, with MagSafe through the camera-half back and the titanium hinge left exposed when folded ([Apple Duo Case Sand](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr94zm/a/iphone-duo-case-sand); [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/)). Post-announce reporting adds that the Case uses adhesive strips along inside edges because the open halves are thin; MagSafe magnets appear only in the camera-half piece; the upper cover-display half has none ([MacRumors adhesive article](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/16/iphone-duo-case-adhesive/); [YouTube first-look](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-2qEsrthOuo)). Apple publishes **no** installed thickness or weight for either case; Amazon and carrier “product dimensions” and weights look like packaging (for example Folio listing width **0.57 in** / Case **0.72 in**) and must not be used as MagSafe air-gap inputs ([Mac Observer](https://www.macobserver.com/news/iphone-duo-folio-case-prices/); [Amazon Duo Case Sand](https://www.amazon.com/Apple-iPhone-Duo-Case-complement/dp/B0HJB6RRQ9/); [Amazon Folio Taupe](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HJB96WQK)). FoldifyCase’s engineering estimate of roughly **1 mm** wall per face (~**+2 mm** closed, ~**13.3 mm** pocket stack) is derived, not measured on Apple’s case ([FoldifyCase dimensions](https://www.foldifycase.com/blogs/news/iphone-duo-confirmed-specs-case-fit-2026)).

Dock failure modes follow from those two envelopes. Flush MagSafe desk mounts collide with a deployed Folio kickstand or block Apple’s documented under-stand charge path ([Apple Folio Taupe](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe)). Clamps sized to cased glass can still crush the exposed hinge cover when the Case is installed ([Apple Duo Case Sand](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr94zm/a/iphone-duo-case-sand)). Folio closed hides the outer display under a flap, so docks that assume an always-visible cover screen fail when the flap is shut. Any cradle must declare which of closed, book-open, and Folio-kickstand-propped states it supports, because bare depth alone swings from **5.2 mm** to **11.3 mm** before case extras ([Apple iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/)).

## Public MagSafe Attach numbers fill the MFi drawing gap for magnets

Apple’s free **Accessory Design Guidelines for Apple Devices** (updated **2026-09-21**) is the authoritative open mechanical source for MagSafe Attach even though Duo-specific phone drawings are absent from the public index ([Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf); [Apple Developer Accessories](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/)). Chapter **42 MagSafe Attach** publishes case and accessory magnet-array geometry: case and accessory rings share outer diameter **54.10 mm** and inner diameter **46.00 mm**; case magnets are **N45SH NdFeB** with surface gauss ≤ **0.215 T**; accessory magnets are **N48H NdFeB** with the same gauss ceiling; MagSafe accessories must self-align within **1.55 mm** radial maximum; MagSafe cases claiming MagSafe or Qi wireless power **2.0+** must integrate the MagSafe Case Magnet Array, enclose the device, and keep uniform thickness ≤ **2.1 mm** (Apple recommends **2.0 mm**) over the array ([Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)). Case-to-charger pull-force targets sit at **1000–1300 gf** normal to the case back; accessory-to-device pull-force spans **650–1510 gf** depending on configuration ([Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)). Dock-related case bottom buildup must not exceed **1.8 mm** for dock compatibility, and MagSafe Charger Mounts (Chapter **22**) hold Apple’s puck rather than enclosing the phone the way case magnet arrays do ([Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)).

MFi program scope separates licensing from those public drawings. Apple’s MFi FAQ states that protective cases and folios that do not integrate MFi components are **outside** MFi, and that developers of non-electronic accessories need not join—Apple points them to the Accessory Design Guidelines instead ([mfi.apple.com FAQs](https://mfi.apple.com/en/faqs.html)). Inductive transmitters must be Qi Certified to Power Class 0 Spec Version **2.0 or later** (Apple recommends **2.2 or later**) under Device Power (Inductive) rules ([Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)). WPC’s Qi2 Magnetic Power Profile is the industry magnetic-alignment standard built on MagSafe-class attach; Qi2 **25 W** (Qi v2.2.1) launched July 2025, while the latest full Qi specification (**v2.3**) and test books remain WPC-member gated ([WPC Qi Wireless charging](https://www.wirelesspowerconsortium.com/standards/qi-wireless-charging/); [Qi2 25W news release](https://www.wirelesspowerconsortium.com/media/wvsmia32/qi2-25w-news-release-v72.pdf); [WPC Qi specs download](https://www.wirelesspowerconsortium.com/knowledge-base/qi-specification/download-the-qi-specifications/)). Market language “MagSafe compatible” (magnets only) is not the same as “Made for MagSafe” licensed charging ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/334301/buyer-beware-magsafe-compatible-is-not-the-same-as-made-for-magsafe.html)). Practical matrix for makers: 3D-printed puck stands follow Chapter **22**; passive magnet wallets and MagSafe-compatible cases follow Chapter **42** without MFi; Qi2/MagSafe charger products need WPC Qi certification plus EMC/FCC and MagSafe licensing for branded high-power claims ([mfi.apple.com FAQs](https://mfi.apple.com/en/faqs.html); [Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf)).

FCC filings for parent model **A3447** under FCC ID **BCG-E9188A** (regional variants A3719/A3720/A3721) establish RF/WPT compliance identity but were not mined here for mechanical CAD ([FCC test report summary](https://fccid.ai/m/1396489ceb5b1892cdef9af94da442acb8356f9e2d6d4dba4079ecbade61f5b3.pdf)). Engadget notes that Apple supplies manufacturers with dimensional drawings that explain why legacy cases do not fit—those partner packages remain gated, not on the public download index ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2281471/iphone-duo-which-accessories-fit/)).

## Conclusion

Accessory design for iPhone Duo is a two-state chassis problem plus a two-case envelope problem, not a single slab-phone MagSafe port. High-confidence CAD can start from Apple’s open and closed millimeters, MagSafe Attach public ring diameters and case-thickness ceilings, a camera-half-only magnet plane, and closed-preferred upright MagSafe mounting with a ledge if open use is required. Folio-compatible docks must reserve under-kickstand MagSafe clearance and tolerate fabric flap thickness; Case-compatible docks must tolerate adhesive two-shell edge lips and an exposed hinge. Precision lips, camera cutouts, MagSafe plate centers, and phone-plus-case Z-stacks remain low-confidence until Apple publishes Duo dimensional drawings or retail caliper datasets after the late-October 2026 launch window—community STLs that reuse Apple’s overall dims are visualization only, and “MagSafe Duo” Printables models are the wrong product.
