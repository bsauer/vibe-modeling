## MagSafe attachment and through-case / kickstand charging (Qi2 25W / Qi)

### Takeaway
Both official cases claim built-in magnets aligned to iPhone Duo for MagSafe attach and through-case wireless charging; Folio uniquely allows MagSafe/Qi2/Qi charging **on the outside or behind the open kickstand**.

### Cited Findings
- **Both cases (Newsroom):** “Both cases are compatible with MagSafe, Qi2 25W, and Qi-certified chargers. With built-in magnets that align perfectly with iPhone Duo, they offer a magical attach experience and fast wireless charging.” — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/).
- **Folio charging (Apple Store):** “When it’s time to charge, just leave the case on your device and snap a MagSafe charger or Qi2 25W or Qi-certified charger **on the outside or behind the open kickstand** to charge.” — [Apple Folio Taupe](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe).
- **Duo Case charging (Apple Store):** “When it’s time to charge, just leave the case on your device and snap on a MagSafe charger, or set it on a Qi2 25W or Qi-certified charger.” — [Apple Duo Case Sand](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr94zm/a/iphone-duo-case-sand).
- MacRumors paraphrase: Folio can charge via MagSafe “behind the kickstand, even when it’s propped open.” — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/iphone-duo-gets-its-own-case-and-folio/).
- Bare **iPhone Duo** wireless: “MagSafe wireless charging up to 25W”; “Qi2 wireless charging up to 25W”; includes magnet array, alignment magnet, Accessory Identification NFC, magnetometer — [Apple Duo Specs](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/).
- Bare Duo wireless half-charge claim: wirelessly charge up to 50% in around 30 minutes with MagSafe or Qi2 (footnote testing with MagSafe Chargers A3502/A3503 and 40W Dynamic Power Adapter) — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/).
- MagSafe Charger support article: system optimizes at “up to 25W of peak power delivery”; plug into power before placing iPhone; do not place cards/badges between phone and charger; MagSafe + wired simultaneously prefers connector charging — [Apple Support 105047](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105047). *(Note: model list in that article excerpt did not explicitly enumerate iPhone Duo in the scraped text.)*
- MagSafe **case** design guideline (MFi Accessory Design Guidelines): cases integrating MagSafe case magnet array shall enclose the device; uniform thickness **≤ 2.1 mm** (Apple recommends 2.0 mm); firmly attach without relying solely on magnets; MagSafe accessories shall self-align within **1.55 mm radial** maximum; surface gauss ≤ 0.215 T — [Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf).
- Dock-related case rule: “The distance from bottom of the device to the outside of a case should not exceed **1.8 mm** for compatibility with docks.” — [Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf).
- MagSafe **accessory** magnet arrays “shall **not** enclose the device” (distinct from case arrays) — [Accessory Design Guidelines PDF](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf).

### Inferences
- Folio’s “outside **or** behind open kickstand” wording implies the MagSafe coil/magnet plane remains usable in two geometries: flat kickstand-stowed attach, and charger nested under the raised stand foot—critical for dock/stand designers who leave a pocket under a deployed kickstand.
- Duo Case charging copy does **not** mention a kickstand path; design docks for Case as a conventional MagSafe-through-case stack.
- Official Apple case thickness is unpublished; whether Folio/Case meet the MFi ≤2.1 mm MagSafe-case thickness rule cannot be verified from Apple’s consumer pages—only that Apple claims MagSafe/Qi2/Qi through-case charging works.

### Gaps
- No published wattage derating table for Duo+official-case vs bare Duo.
- No measured coil-to-coil air gap or magnet-ring Z-offset with either case installed.
- Apple Support MagSafe model matrix may lag Duo launch; treat phone specs + Store case claims as authoritative for Duo-specific through-case claims.

## Clearances and failure modes for docks / plates / MagSafe mounts claiming “works with Apple cases”

### Takeaway
Designers must treat Folio and Case as **two different envelopes**: Folio adds kickstand volume and under-stand MagSafe nesting; Case adds two-part edge lips with hinge exposure. Use Apple’s general MagSafe case ≤2.1 mm / 1.55 mm alignment / 1.8 mm dock-bottom rules as MFi ceilings—not as measured Duo case geometry.

### Cited Findings
- Folio uniquely requires MagSafe clearance **behind open kickstand** per Apple Store charging copy — [Apple Folio](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe).
- Both cases claim MagSafe self-align and through-case Qi2 25W / Qi — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/).
- MFi MagSafe case thickness ceiling 2.1 mm (recommend 2.0 mm); radial alignment 1.55 mm; case must enclose device and not depend solely on magnets for retention — [Accessory Design Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf).
- Dock compatibility: case bottom buildup ≤ 1.8 mm — [Accessory Design Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf).
- MagSafe charger mounts/accessories must not enclose the device (accessory vs case magnet arrays) — [Accessory Design Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf).
- Thin cases ≤2.25 mm vs thick cases >2.25 mm have different acoustic-port rules (relevant if a dock or plate occludes speakers/mics) — [Accessory Design Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/Accessory-Design-Guidelines.pdf).
- MagSafe Support: foreign objects (cards, badges) between charger and phone can damage strips/RFID and impair charging; heat may throttle charging — [Apple Support 105047](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105047).
- Case folded alt text: titanium hinge exposed (Case) — dock fingers that clamp the hinge may contact bare metal even “with Apple Case” — [Apple Duo Case](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr94zm/a/iphone-duo-case-sand).
- Folio flap covers entire outer display when closed — [Apple Folio alt text](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mkr64zm/a/iphone-duo-folio-with-kickstand-taupe); docks that assume always-visible outer display fail when Folio is closed.
- Phone open thickness 5.2 mm vs closed 11.3 mm — [Apple Duo Specs](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/); any cradle must accommodate **both** folded and unfolded states **plus** case/Folio extras.

### Inferences — design failure modes (for accessory CAD)
1. **Kickstand collision:** MagSafe desk mounts that sit flush against the Folio back will collide with a deployed kickstand or block Apple’s documented under-stand charge path.
2. **Single-envelope claim:** “Works with Apple Duo cases” that only test the $79 Case will fail Folio flap thickness, fabric friction, and kickstand depth.
3. **Hinge clamp:** Cases leave hinge visible; clamps sized to cased glass may crush or scratch the exposed titanium hinge cover.
4. **Alignment stack:** Extra MagSafe plates under a case can exceed effective magnet/coil gap even if Apple’s own cases charge; MFi 2.1 mm is a case-body ceiling, not a free budget for third-party add-ons.
5. **Fold state:** Open 5.2 mm vs closed 11.3 mm bare depth means vertical docks must declare which state(s) they support; Folio kickstand adds a third propped state.
6. **Bottom dock:** Any Lightning/USB-era 1.8 mm bottom-build rule still appears in current Accessory Design Guidelines—USB-C Duo docks should still avoid thick bottom bezels if claiming Apple-case dock compatibility.
7. **Third-party naming trap:** Products named “iPhone Duo” MagSafe stand are **not** these cases; compatibility claims must name Folio SKUs (MKR64/MKR74) and Case SKUs (MKR84/MKR94) separately.

### Gaps
- No Apple-published MagSafe Z-height or ring OD specific to iPhone Duo vs slab iPhones in the consumer docs scraped here (dimensional drawings may exist behind Apple Developer download gates).
- No failure-mode data from retail returns or lab drop tests for either official case as of this note.
- No verified whether Folio kickstand contains ferrous parts that could trigger MagSafe FOD if a charger is mis-placed against the stand leaf rather than the MagSafe window Apple describes.
