# Current Apple Pencil lineup (late October 2026)

Research as-of: 2026-10-22. US Apple Store / apple.com prices and Support tech-specs. Prefer Apple primary pages over Wikipedia when they conflict.

## Which Pencils are on the live Apple Store accessories catalog today?

### Takeaway
Apple still sells **four** Apple Pencil models new on apple.com, plus replacement tips and the USB-C-to-Pencil adapter: Pencil Pro, Pencil (USB-C), Pencil (2nd generation), and Pencil (1st generation). None of these four is marked discontinued on the live catalog.

### Cited Findings
- The overview page lists Buy links for Pro (`MX2D3`), USB-C (`MUWA3`), 2nd gen (`MXN43`), and 1st gen (`MYQW3`) — [Apple Pencil overview](https://www.apple.com/apple-pencil/).
- The iPad accessories Apple Pencil category lists the same four pencils plus Tips 4-pack — [iPad Accessories · Apple Pencil](https://www.apple.com/shop/ipad/accessories/apple-pencil).
- Live US prices: Pro **$129.00**, USB-C **$79.00**, 2nd gen **$129.00**, 1st gen **$99.00**, Tips 4-pack **$19.00** — [Pro product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mx2d3am/a/apple-pencil-pro); [USB-C product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/muwa3am/a/apple-pencil-usb-c); [2nd gen product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mxn43am/a/apple-pencil-2nd-generation); [1st gen product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/myqw3am/a/apple-pencil-1st-generation); [Tips](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mx763am/a/apple-pencil-tips-4-pack).
- Select flow highlights Pro and USB-C as primary choices, with separate links still selling 1st and 2nd gen — [Select Apple Pencil](https://www.apple.com/shop/select-apple-pencil).
- USB-C to Apple Pencil Adapter sold separately for **$9.00** (US part `MWML3AM/A`) — [Adapter product](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mwml3am/a/usb-c-to-apple-pencil-adapter).
- Refurbished USB-C also listed (`FUWA3AM/A`) — [Refurbished USB-C](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/fuwa3am/a/refurbished-apple-pencil-usb-c).

### Inferences
- For “sold today / new by Apple,” treat all four pens as **current**, not discontinued. 1st/2nd gen remain catalog SKUs for older iPads even though marketing emphasizes Pro and USB-C.
- No fifth Pencil model appears in the live accessories catalog.

### Gaps
- Apple does not publish an explicit “discontinued” badge on Support tech-specs for historical pens; discontinuation status here is inferred from continued Buy / shop listing presence.
- Non-US regional part suffixes (e.g. `MUWA3ZM/A` UK) were not exhaustively inventoried.

Apple’s Support tech-specs and shop pages agree on diameter **8.9 mm (0.35 in)** for all four current models. Lengths differ: Pro/2nd gen 166 mm, USB-C 155 mm, 1st gen 175.7 mm tip-to-cap. Wikipedia’s USB-C diameter **7.4 mm** conflicts with Apple and should be ignored for design work.

- Wikipedia lists USB-C diameter as **0.29 in (7.4 mm)** — [Wikipedia · Apple Pencil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Pencil); contradicted by Apple’s **8.9 mm** on [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318) and shop pages.

### Inferences
- Pro and 2nd gen share the same outer length/diameter envelope; weight differs (Pro heavier: 19.15 g vs 18.2 g).
- USB-C is shorter (155 mm) at the same stated diameter.

### Gaps
- Apple does not publish flat-edge chord dimensions, magnet spacing, or tip-protrusion beyond overall length.
- Tip thread pitch / major diameter is not on tech-specs (see Tips section).

## How each charges and pairs (Lightning, USB-C sliding cap, magnetic wireless)

### Takeaway
Charging/pairing splits cleanly: **1st gen = Lightning** (plus USB-C adapter path on USB-C iPads); **USB-C = cable via sliding-cap USB-C** (magnetic storage only); **2nd gen and Pro = magnetic attach that pairs and wirelessly charges**.

### Cited Findings
- **Pro:** “attaches, pairs, and charges magnetically on the side of your iPad”; Connections: Bluetooth; Other: Magnetically attaches, pairs and charges — [Support 120123](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123); [overview](https://www.apple.com/apple-pencil/).
- **USB-C:** “pairs and charges via a USB-C Charge Cable”; “magnetically attaches to the side of iPad for **storage**”; Connections: Bluetooth + USB-C Connector; feature table marks wireless pairing/charging as not supported — [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318); [overview feature matrix](https://www.apple.com/apple-pencil/).
- **2nd gen:** “pairs, charges, and stores magnetically”; Connections: Bluetooth; Other: Magnetically attaches and pairs — [Support 111889](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111889); [shop](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mxn43am/a/apple-pencil-2nd-generation).
- **1st gen:** “pairs and charges via Lightning”; Connections: Bluetooth + Lightning; magnetically attached **cap** (not side-of-iPad magnetic charge) — [Support 111920](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111920).
- Pairing recovery: Pro/2nd gen — center on magnetic connector; 1st gen Lightning iPads — plug into Lightning; 1st gen + iPad (10th gen) — USB-C to Apple Pencil Adapter + USB-C cable — [Support 108788](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108788).
- Identify/serial: Pro / 2nd gen / USB-C serial under tip; 1st gen serial on Lightning connector after removing cap — [Support 109031](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109031).
- System requirements: Pro iPadOS 17.5+; USB-C iPadOS 17.1.1+ — [Support 120123](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123); [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318).

### Inferences
- USB-C sliding end-cap reveals the USB-C port (marketing imagery / overview copy); magnetic attach does **not** charge or auto-pair that model.
- Pro and 2nd gen share the magnetic charge/pair UX; Pro adds squeeze/haptics/Find My/etc.

### Gaps
- Support article [120390](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120390) (“How to connect and pair…”) returned mostly a video thumbnail shell via fetch; detailed step text was not fully extracted—use [108788](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108788) for pairing mechanics.
- Exact wireless-charge power / attach force not published.

## Any Pencil support for iPhone Duo or only iPad?

### Takeaway
**Only Apple Pencil (USB-C)** is listed for iPhone Duo. Pro, 2nd gen, and 1st gen remain iPad-only on Apple’s Pencil compatibility materials. Duo tech specs state display support for Pencil (USB-C); marketing FAQ has said support is “coming later this year.”

### Cited Findings
- USB-C tech specs **Compatibility → iPhone Models: iPhone Duo** (plus full iPad list) — [Support 121318](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121318); same on [shop MUWA3AM/A](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/muwa3am/a/apple-pencil-usb-c).
- Pro / 2nd gen / 1st gen tech-specs and shop compatibility sections list **iPad models only** (no iPhone) — [120123](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123); [111889](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111889); [111920](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111920).
- iPhone Duo specs: Both displays “Supports Apple Pencil (USB-C)” (footnote 3) — [iPhone Duo Technical Specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/).
- iPhone Duo FAQ (marketing): “Does iPhone Duo work with Apple Pencil or a stylus? Yes. iPhone Duo will work with Apple Pencil (USB‑C). Coming later this year.” — [iPhone Duo](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/).
- Apple Support “Apple Pencil compatibility” ([108937](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108937), published Mar 10, 2026) lists **only iPad models** for all four pencils and does **not** mention iPhone Duo — conflicts in scope with USB-C tech specs / Duo specs which do list Duo.
- Secondary reporting: Duo limited to USB-C Pencil, not Pro — [MacRumors 2026-09-09](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-pencil-usb-c-iphone-duo/); iOS 27.2 enablement claims — [MacRumors 2026-10-21](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/21/iphone-duo-apple-pencil-support-ios-27-2/) (not Apple primary).

### Inferences
- For accessory design against Duo, assume **USB-C Pencil only**; no magnetic Pencil charge surface implied on phone.
- Prefer USB-C tech specs + Duo specs over the iPad-only [108937](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108937) article for phone compatibility.

### Gaps
- Exact iOS version / ship date when Duo Pencil support goes live was not confirmed on an Apple Support KB as of this scrape (FAQ: “later this year”; secondary press cites iOS 27.2).
- Whether Duo supports hover / which Pencil features on phone displays is not detailed on Pencil tech-specs beyond listing the device.

- Battery capacity / Wh not on current Support tech-specs for these four.
